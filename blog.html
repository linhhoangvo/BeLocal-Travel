COUNTRYSIDE · HOI AN

Beyond the Old Town

Hoi An’s familiar streets are a lovely place to begin. When you have a little more time, a bicycle ride takes you toward quieter village paths, green fields and the everyday work happening beside the river.

Leave enough space in the day to stop and talk. A short visit with a family or craftsperson can tell you more about a place than a hurried list of landmarks. The ride itself is part of the pleasure: slower roads, different sounds and a view of the town from outside its centre.

FOOD & CULTURE · HOI AN

A slower morning in Hoi An

Sometimes the best plan is a simple one: take a different street, find a place to sit and give yourself time for coffee. Watch the rhythm around you before moving on to the next stop.

A café can be a small doorway into local life. Notice the conversations, the way a drink is prepared and the streets just outside. There is no need to cover the whole town in one morning.
