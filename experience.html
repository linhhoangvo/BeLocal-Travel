What to look forward to

    How the evening unfolds

    Meet your guide at 26 Phan Boi Chau. Walk through the Old Town to two cocktail stops, a street food stop, a café for dessert, and a final local bar. The tour ends back at the meeting point.

    Included

    Drinks and food served as part of the tour, plus an English-speaking local guide. Vegetarian food and non-alcoholic drinks are available.

    Before you book

    For adults aged 18 and over. Extra orders, personal expenses, and tips are not included. The exact stops may change; check the booking details for your date.
