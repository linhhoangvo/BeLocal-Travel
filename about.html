— MEET BE LOCAL

Two sisters.
One home in Hoi An.

Linh and Rosy grew up in Quang Binh and have called Hoi An home for the last ten years.

— OUR STORY

A place becomes home through the people you meet.

We came to Hoi An from Quang Binh, a place shaped by strong landscapes, generous people and a quiet kind of resilience. Over the last decade, Hoi An has become more than somewhere we live. It is where our work, friendships and everyday routines have grown.

We started Be Local because the Hoi An we love is not only the lanterns and the old streets. It is the family who keeps a craft alive, the cook who knows exactly when a dish is ready, the rider who takes the long way through the countryside and the café owner who remembers how you take your coffee.

Our experiences are small by design. We want you to have time to ask questions, share a meal and notice the details that are easy to miss when you are moving too quickly.

— HOW WE TRAVEL

Go beyond the landmark

We look for the streets, workshops and tables where daily life continues after the sightseeing route ends.

Meet people, not just places

Local hosts and guides are part of the story. Their knowledge gives each experience its character.

Leave room for the day

A good tour should feel unhurried, personal and open to the small moments you did not plan for.
