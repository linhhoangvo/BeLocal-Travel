— OUR STORY

Who we are

Be Local Vietnam was founded by Thao and Huy, two travel professionals who share a passion for creating meaningful experiences that benefit both travellers and local communities.

Thao has worked in Vietnam’s tourism industry since the early 2000s. With more than 20 years of experience as a product manager and tour operator, she has travelled extensively throughout Vietnam to inspect destinations, develop unique itineraries and build strong relationships with local communities.

Huy has always believed that the best journeys are built around people. Combining his passion for travel with a background in building high-performing teams and technology-driven travel experiences, he brings a fresh perspective to modern tourism.

Together, they created Be Local with one simple mission: to help travellers experience the real Vietnam through genuine local connections, hands-on experiences and responsible travel.
