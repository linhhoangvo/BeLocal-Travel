— OUR STORY

A place becomes home through the people you meet.

We came to Hoi An from Quang Binh, a place shaped by strong landscapes, generous people and a quiet kind of resilience. Over the last decade, Hoi An has become more than somewhere we live. It is where our work, friendships and everyday routines have grown.

We started Be Local because the Hoi An we love is not only the lanterns and the old streets. It is the family who keeps a craft alive, the cook who knows exactly when a dish is ready, the rider who takes the long way through the countryside and the café owner who remembers how you take your coffee.

Our experiences are small by design. We want you to have time to ask questions, share a meal and notice the details that are easy to miss when you are moving too quickly.

— HOW WE TRAVEL

Go beyond the landmark

We look for the streets, workshops and tables where daily life continues after the sightseeing route ends.

Meet people, not just places

Local hosts and guides are part of the story. Their knowledge gives each experience its character.

Leave room for the day

A good tour should feel unhurried, personal and open to the small moments you did not plan for.

— MEET THE FOUNDER

Rosie Hoa Duong

Founder & Managing Director

Xin chào! I’m Hoa.

My name means flower in Vietnamese. My mother says a flower asks for nothing and gives everything: colour, fragrance and a reason to look up.

I have spent the last fifteen years learning what it means to travel well. It began as a guide, walking travellers through morning markets still damp with dew, past rice fields turning gold, and listening more than I spoke. Later came a Master’s in International Sustainable Tourism Management at Monash University and an MBA in Australia.

But the further I travelled, the more I fell in love with home. Be Local grows from that love—not the Vietnam of postcards, but the Vietnam of rice fields before sunrise, family kitchens, skilled hands and quiet village evenings.

I don’t believe a journey should simply move you from one place to the next. I believe it should move something in you: quieter, more present and closer to the people and fleeting moments you are lucky enough to share.

— MEET THE TEAM

Linh Vo

Operations Manager

Hi, I’m Linh.

I was born in Quang Binh and have called Hoi An home for the past ten years. This little town has a way of making you slow down, notice the small things and fall in love with Vietnam a little more each day.

I have always loved travelling for more than beautiful places. It is the people you meet, the food you remember and the little moments that stay with you long after a trip ends.

At Be Local Travel, I take care of the details behind each experience so our guests can feel relaxed, supported and present while they are here.

For me, the best kind of travel feels effortless, personal and full of heart. That is what I hope every guest takes home from Hoi An.
