Home is something
We share.

We’re both from Quang Binh, and for the past ten years we’ve called Hoi An home. Over time, its familiar faces, quiet lanes and everyday rhythms have become part of our lives.

Rosie has spent years sharing Vietnam with travellers, always finding that the best stories come from taking time to listen. Linh looks after the little details that help people feel comfortable and enjoy the moment.

We started Be Local because we’d love to share the Hoi An we know with you: the people we’ve come to know, the places we return to and the simple moments that make this town feel like home.

Rosie

Founder

Known to family as Hoa, meaning flower, Rosie brings years of guiding and a belief that travel can offer connection, care and a new way of seeing home.

Linh

Operations & guest experience

Linh looks after the details behind each journey, giving guests room to notice Hoi An’s people, food and small moments.

The little things make the difference.

For us, sustainable travel begins with the way we meet people and move through a place.

People first

Make time to meet the families, artists and neighbours who give a place its character.

Take part

Learn a craft, share a meal or follow a village path at an easy pace.

Travel with care

Move gently through each place, respect local traditions and support the families and makers who welcome us.

Keep it personal

Small groups leave space for questions, conversation and unexpected moments.
