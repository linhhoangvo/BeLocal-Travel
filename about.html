— OUR STORY

From Quang Binh
to Hoi An.

Two sisters, one place to call home, and a shared love of showing travellers the Vietnam we know.

— ROSY & LINH

Home is something we share.

We grew up in Quang Binh, where the landscape stays with you: fields after rain, familiar voices at the market, the kind of welcome that makes a visitor feel like family. Ten years ago, Hoi An became our home. Over time, its quiet lanes, neighbourhood cafés and the people we met here became part of our everyday lives.

Rosy found her way into travel as a guide, walking alongside guests through markets and villages and learning to listen to the stories behind each place. Her work later took her further across Vietnam and to Australia to study sustainable tourism and business. The more she travelled, the more she wanted to share the warmth and depth of home.

For Linh, the joy is in the details: a meal someone remembers long after leaving, a relaxed conversation with a local maker, a day that flows easily because someone has cared for everything behind the scenes. She brings that care to each experience so guests can slow down and be present.

Be Local grew from the place we now call home. We want to take you beyond the postcard view of Hoi An and introduce you to the people, traditions and small moments that make it feel alive. Come curious. We’ll make room for the unexpected.
