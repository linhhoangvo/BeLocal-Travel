Beyond the Old Town: Hoi An by bicycle

The Old Town is often where a visit begins. The quieter roads beyond it offer a different sense of place: gardens, riverside homes, workshops and families going about their day. A bicycle gives you time to notice the distance between those moments.

The Old Town is often where a visit begins. The quieter roads beyond it offer a different sense of place: gardens, riverside homes, workshops and families going about their day. A bicycle gives you time to notice the distance between those moments.

A ride towards Tra Nhieu Village is about more than scenery. Local families continue to make things by hand, including rice paper and woven mats. A guide can introduce the people behind the work and explain how these crafts fit into daily life. If you are invited to try, take your time. The point is to understand the work, rather than rush to the next stop.

Leave space for lunch as well. Sitting down with a family changes the rhythm of the day, and a boat journey along the Thu Bon River makes a calm way back to town.

Before you go: You need to be comfortable riding a bicycle. Wear shoes you can pedal in, bring sun protection and check the pickup area and weather with your guide.
