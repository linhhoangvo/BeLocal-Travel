When Hoi An comes out after dark

Lantern light changes the feel of Hoi An, but the evening is not limited to its best-known streets. A walk with a local guide can connect the food, drinks and small venues you might otherwise pass by.

Lantern light changes the feel of Hoi An, but the evening is not limited to its best-known streets. A walk with a local guide can connect the food, drinks and small venues you might otherwise pass by.

The pleasure is in the changes of pace: a cocktail in a tucked-away bar, something savoury at a food stop, a sweet pause at a café and one last drink before the night ends. Moving on foot lets each stop feel like part of the town, rather than a separate destination.

You do not need to know the local bar scene before arriving. Tell your guide what you like to eat and drink, and ask about vegetarian food or non-alcoholic options if you prefer them. Exact venues and menus can change, so leave a little room for surprise.

Before you go: The cocktail experience is for adults aged 18 and over. Check the start time, meeting point and what is included on the booking page.
